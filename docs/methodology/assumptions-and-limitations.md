# Assumptions and Limitations

- CSVs may not contain all inputs needed for emissions estimation.
- Native provider exports differ and may change.
- Factors vary by geography, period, service, and methodology.
- Carbon estimates may differ from provider-reported values.
- Cost is not a direct proxy for energy use or emissions.
- Recommendations require engineering review.
- Approval is feedback only; it does not execute cloud changes.
- Synthetic data is not evidence of real-world impact.
- This MVP is not an audited inventory or compliance tool.
