# Secondary Research Log

## Purpose
Understand cloud-financial-management practices, carbon-estimation approaches, and existing tools. Secondary research does not prove that a target segment will adopt CarbonLens.

## Initial sources
1. FinOps Foundation — State of FinOps: https://data.finops.org/
2. Cloud Carbon Footprint — Methodology: https://www.cloudcarbonfootprint.org/docs/methodology/
3. AWS Sustainability methodology: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sustainability/latest/userguide/methodology.html
4. Google Cloud Carbon Footprint methodology: https://docs.cloud.google.com/carbon-footprint/docs/methodology

## Research template
Record title, publisher, URL, date, method/sample, exact finding, context, limits, product implication, confidence, and access date.

## Working hypotheses
- H1: Users need actionable prioritization beyond a raw emissions figure.
- H2: Source transparency and uncertainty labels improve trust.
- H3: Human-reviewed recommendations are more appropriate for the MVP than automatic changes.

These are hypotheses, not confirmed user findings.
